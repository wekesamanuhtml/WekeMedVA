About me

I’m a HIPAA-certified healthcare administrative support specialist with two years of experience as a medical virtual assistant. I work with solo practitioners, group clinics, telehealth startups, and specialists, handling the day-to-day admin that keeps a practice running smoothly: patient intake, scheduling, insurance verification, prior authorizations, and more.

I work in US hours and am comfortable in the major practice management and EHR platforms, so I can get up to speed quickly and become a dependable extension of your team.
